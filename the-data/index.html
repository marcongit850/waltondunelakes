Water quality

THE DATA

This page summarizes publicly presented water-quality education for Walton County’s coastal dune lakes. It draws on the 2025 Coastal Dune Lake Water Quality Update from Kayla Wingard, CBAEP (CDLAB presentation, September 30, 2025).

Live monitoring and the official dashboards stay with the Choctawhatchee Basin Alliance (CBA). This site is an independent educational guide, and it is not the official source for these measurements.
