# Photograph credits

Photographs are stored in this repository and served from local paths. A picture is named for a specific Walton County dune lake only when the source names that lake, or when the frame is centered on a published coordinate for that lake.

The service copyright line is “USGS, USDA, The National Map: Orthoimagery.” The imagery is a work of the U.S. Government and is in the public domain.

## Ground-photo search

Wikimedia Commons (title search, place categories, and a geotagged search within 2 km of each lake), Openverse, and Flickr results inside Openverse were searched for a freely licensed ground photograph of each lake. Files that name a different lake were rejected, including Alligator Lake in Columbia County and Redfish Lake in Idaho.

Verified ground photographs that name the water:

- Western Lake: the shore, a second shore view at the state park, the County Road 30A bridge, and the park-entrance bridge. All four are in the Western Lake gallery. The County Road 30A bridge is also on the home page. A fish-trap buoy file names Western Lake in the filename (`File:Fish trap buoy with reflection in Western Lake.jpg`, Paul and Jill, CC BY 2.0); its Commons description says only Grayton Beach State Park, so it is not used.
- Lake Powell: the bridge at Camp Helen State Park, in the gallery. A second Camp Helen frame is the park marsh, captioned as the marsh.
- Oyster Lake: a green heron at the outflow, in the gallery, captioned as the bird at the cut.

No freely licensed ground photograph turned up for Fuller, Morris, Campbell, Stallworth, Allen, Draper, Big Redfish, Little Redfish, Alligator, Eastern, the surface of Deer Lake, or Camp Creek that the file text identifies as that lake. Where a Commons file clearly shows the park or beach beside that lake, it is in the gallery with a caption that names the real place. Fuller, Stallworth, Allen, Draper, Big Redfish, and Camp Creek have no such extra, so those pages show only the aerial.
