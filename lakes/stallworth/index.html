Stallworth Lake

Lake 4 of 15, west to east · Dune Allen

Aerial photograph of Stallworth Lake in Walton County, Florida, from the U.S. Geological Survey National Agriculture Imagery Program. The frame is centered on the USGS geographic-names point recorded as Stalworth Lake. Photo by U.S. Geological Survey, public domain, via the USGS National Map.

About

Stallworth Lake is a small coastal dune lake, about 15 acres, in Dune Allen. Much of the shoreline is private. It is easier to think of it as a place to watch birds than as a lake with a formal public launch.

Its size is part of the lesson of the chain. Coastal dune lakes are not all broad public waters. Some are quiet basins tucked into a developed coast, still opening intermittently toward the Gulf, with little of the shore in public hands.

Public access

No formal public launch is described here. The shoreline is largely private. Bird-watching is the public use this guide can point to. Do not assume a yard, dock, or beach path is open.

Access rules, park fees, and reservation systems change. This page is a summary for orientation, not a permit or a promise that a shore is open.
