The lakes

Fifteen named coastal dune lakes, from Fuller Lake at Topsail to Lake Powell on the line between Walton County and Bay County.

Grayton Beach State Park. Photo by Paul and Jill, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

The map is a guide. The cards are the index. Either one opens a lake page.

WestScroll if the coast is cut offEast

Select a lake on the map or in the cards. West is left and east is right.

  • West is left and east is right. Inland is toward the top. The Gulf is toward the bottom.
  • Numbers match the cards. Marker size suggests relative surface area, not a surveyed outline.
  • Dashed lines are schematic intermittent outfalls. Fuller Lake has no direct outfall. A short link joins it to Morris Lake.
  • Western Lake’s line is heavier because its outfall is the best known on this coast. It is still intermittent. Allen Lake’s line stands for drainage under County Road 30A.

Places, west to east: Topsail Hill (1–3), Dune Allen (4–6), Blue Mountain (7–8), Grayton (9–11), Seagrove (12), Deer Lake (13), Camp Creek (14), and the Walton–Bay county line (15).
