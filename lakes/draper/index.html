Draper Lake

Lake 7 of 15, west to east · Blue Mountain Beach

Aerial photograph of Draper Lake in Walton County, Florida, from the U.S. Geological Survey National Agriculture Imagery Program, centered on the USGS geographic-names point for this lake. Photo by U.S. Geological Survey, public domain, via the USGS National Map.

About

Draper Lake covers about 40 acres near Blue Mountain Beach. Published descriptions put the average depth near 5.9 feet, a figure this guide does not repeat for the other lakes because it was not given for them.

Roads and culverts have been built across paths the water would otherwise take. The basin is still a coastal dune lake. The way water enters it and leaves it has been shaped by the street network. Draper is the clearest short lesson, along this chain, in how a road grid and a dune-lake system occupy the same ground.

Public access

This page does not list a public ramp. Bird-watching is the use described here. The setting to understand is the roads and culverts around Blue Mountain Beach, not a park shore. Private streets and yards are not public access.

Access rules, park fees, and reservation systems change. This page is a summary for orientation, not a permit or a promise that a shore is open.
