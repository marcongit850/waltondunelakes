Little Redfish Lake

Lake 9 of 15, west to east · Grayton Beach State Park area

Aerial photograph of Little Redfish Lake in Walton County, Florida, from the U.S. Geological Survey National Agriculture Imagery Program, centered on the USGS geographic-names point for this lake. Photo by U.S. Geological Survey, public domain, via the USGS National Map.

About

Little Redfish Lake is about 12 acres, in the Grayton Beach State Park area, with the Timpoochee Trail nearby. Small surface area does not make a minor system. The lake, the wetlands around it, and the intermittent path toward the Gulf are still a coastal dune lake, on a compact footprint.

It sits just west of Alligator Lake and the much larger Western Lake. Together those three basins are the Grayton stretch of the chain: one small, one a park gateway, one the iconic multi-lobe lake.

Public access

The lake is in the Grayton Beach State Park area, and the Timpoochee Trail is nearby. This page does not claim a particular ramp on Little Redfish itself, and it does not draw the park boundary. Use marked public access.

Access rules, park fees, and reservation systems change. This page is a summary for orientation, not a permit or a promise that a shore is open.
