Aerial photograph of Little Redfish Lake in Walton County, Florida, from the U.S. Geological Survey National Agriculture Imagery Program, centered on the USGS geographic-names point for this lake. Photo by U.S. Geological Survey, public domain, via the USGS National Map.

About

Little Redfish Lake is about 12 acres, with the Timpoochee Trail nearby. Small surface area does not make a minor system. The lake, the wetlands around it, and the intermittent path toward the Gulf are still a coastal dune lake, on a compact footprint.

It sits just west of Alligator Lake and the much larger Western Lake. Together those three basins sit along a short stretch of the chain: one small, one compact, one the iconic multi-lobe lake.

Public access

Bird-watching is the use described here. This page does not claim a particular ramp on Little Redfish itself.

Access rules, park fees, and reservation systems change. This page is a summary for orientation, not a permit or a promise that a shore is open.
